// The evidence

Why film review works.

Gym vs. game

Practice shows your capability. Games show your reality.

In training, you show what you’re capable of. In games, the pace, pressure and tired legs bring out your habits, and that’s your reality. Your tape is a clear, fair record of it. Look at it together and you can start bridging the gap between your capability and your game-day reality. That gap is where the player you aspire to be gets built.

59%

Even experienced coaches miss 4 in 10 moments

Qualified coaches accurately recalled 59.2% of the critical events in a match they watched, and novices managed 42%. If trained eyes miss that much, memory alone can’t be your feedback. Tape fills the gap.

Blind spot

Without tape, you’re guessing

In the moment you only see a fraction of what happens. Video lets you watch your own movement and decisions at a slower speed, so you can spot habits that feel invisible in real time.
